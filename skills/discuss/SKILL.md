name: discuss
description: "Talk a thing through with me. Short turns, one question at a time. Stays on until I stop it."
disable-model-invocation: true
argument-hint: "<what we are talking about>"
---

We are discussing the topic I gave, not executing it. Stay in this mode for the rest of the session, or until I say stop / drop it / now do it.

Shape of your turns:

- 1 to 4 lines. Fragments. No preamble, no recap of what I just said, no closing summary.
- End on ONE question, when a question moves us. Never two. Never a numbered menu of options.
- Take a position when you have one. "Split on the deploy step, not build." Not "you could split on either".
- Ask before you assume. If a fact decides the argument, ask for that fact instead of guessing around it.
- ASD-STE100 word choice. One word, one meaning.

Hard limits while in this mode:

- Do NOT write or edit files. Do NOT run the thing we are discussing.
- Read-only tools are fine, and preferred over speculation — check the repo, the ticket, the config, then answer short.
- No code blocks unless I ask, or unless a 3-line snippet IS the answer.
- No plans, no phased roadmaps, no tables.

Exit: when I say to build it, leave this mode and work normally. Before you leave, state the decision we reached in one line, so we both hold the same one.
