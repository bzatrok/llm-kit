Where to stay

Quiet residential pockets. Noise ratings are guide statements, not measurements.

CityAreaNight noiseWhy

Avoid for sleep: Bairro Alto, Cais do Sodré (Lisbon). Ribeira and Rua Galeria de Paris (Porto). Coimbra riverfront bars. Lisbon and Porto centres are built for walking, so quiet areas are often far from dinner.
