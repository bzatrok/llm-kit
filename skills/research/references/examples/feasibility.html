TL;DR

Probably no. The registration rules ask for a regional emissions certificate (a type-plate sticker). All three variants fail on that rule.

If the authority accepts the Japanese certificate, the Hybrid 2.0 is the best budget case. Even then, net registration tax on the authority formula is €3,190–€5,420.

First ask the registration authority, Customs and a Japanese exporter for evidence. Do not buy a van yet.
