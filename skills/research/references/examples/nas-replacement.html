NAS replacement

TL;DR

Buy the used Vaultek VK-440+ for €210 shipped. It is the cheapest non-Stornex 4-bay that still gets security updates and takes your 2.5" disks.

Vaultek can end those updates at any time, so plan on 1–2 years (red team). New and low-power: the Brightbyte BB4300 Plus at €350.

Before you pay, ask if the listing includes drive trays and the power adapter.
