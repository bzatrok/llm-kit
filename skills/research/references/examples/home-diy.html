What I got wrong

First photo survey → finding on site.

Guest-shower mixer is thermostatic, knob missing→Manual two-handle mixer. The square stem with a tapped hole shows it.
Basement mixer is thermostatic under a cap→Manual knob. The cartridge under it can still be thermostatic. Run check A.
Any 150 mm mixer fits the wall unions→Brand matters. Nordo and Lumina are 150 mm and still do not fit.
A DIY-chain mixer will do→Delta Bouw, Sanitop and Werfmaat do not publish the cartridge size. Their mixers are not on the union list.

Checks to do on site

Before you order. Check D matters most.

A. Take the basement knob offRound pin that springs back = thermostatic. Square stem = manual. Decides 3 or 4 mixers.Priority 1
B. If thermostatic, caliper the cartridge40.0 mm = standard, fits direct. 38.0 mm = older size, adapter in the box.
C. Look for a pressure reducerAt the water heater or in the installation documents.
D. Measure and magnet-test all 4 wall outletsUnion centres 150 mm, ±6 mm is fine. Steel sticks, copper does not. Decides compression or threaded fit.Priority 2
E. Measure each projection from the wallTempo 300 is 85 ±2 mm. Look for the offset union set, €22.55.
F. Count the spare cartridges4 wanted. Delta Bouw sells only a 4-pack, €89.
G. Hot or cold side in the guest showerA reversed feed means one more connection to rework.
