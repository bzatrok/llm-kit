# Language

Reports are read to make a decision. Every sentence adds a fact, a condition or a consequence. Cut sentences that prepare, restate or reassure.

## Style: close to ASD-STE100

- One idea per sentence. Most sentences under 20 words; instructions under 20 words.
- Active voice, simple tenses. Put the action in the verb.
- Subject and verb next to each other. No clauses in the middle.
- One word for one thing. Do not rotate synonyms. If the option is "TS-451+", it stays "TS-451+".
- Concrete before abstract: name the model, the price, the date, the shop.
- Never drop a number, condition or scope to make a sentence shorter.
- No hedging words in prose. Uncertainty goes in a status chip.
- No "Claude voice": no "Great question", no "Let's dive in", no "It's worth noting", no rhetorical questions, no exclamation marks.

## Controlled status words

Use only these, as chips:

| Word | Meaning | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Verified | Two independent sources, or a primary source | teal |
| Single source | One source only | orange |
| Unverified | Claimed, not found in a source | red |
| Excluded | Fails a hard requirement | neutral |
| Holds / Does not hold | Result of testing a claim or objection | teal / red |

Matrix symbols: ✓ yes, ~ partly or one source only, ? not found, ✗ no. Always show the legend under the matrix.

## TL;DR

- Max 3 lines. Line 1: the answer. Line 2: the main condition or risk. Line 3 (optional): what to do first.
- Mark 1–2 conclusions with `mark`. Link 2–4 key references to their Sources rows.
- No "Pick:" prefix. No next-action banner.

Example: "**Buy the used 4-bay NAS for €190 shipped.** It is the cheapest 4-bay that still gets [security updates] and takes your 2.5" disks."

## Titles and headings

- Report title: 2–4 words, a noun phrase. "NAS replacement", "Portugal 2028".
- Card heading: what the card shows. "Total cost per option", not "Let's look at prices".
- Card subtitle: unit, scope, how it was counted. "Euro, incl. shipping. 12 options with a fixed price."

## Gaps

Title: "What I could not verify". Write each gap as a question. Group by what the answer could change: "Could change the pick", "Could change another option", "Minor". Right column: who can answer ("Ask the seller", "Only you know", "No source found").

## Numbers and locale

- € before the number: €240, €3,411. Ranges with an en dash: €100–145.
- Number format follows the report language: Dutch reports use €2.800 and 0,25; English reports use €2,800 and 0.25. One format per report.
- Metric units with a space: 8 W, 15 mm, 13 h 30.
- Dates: 23 Oct 2026. In tables, 23 Oct is enough when the year is in the meta line.
- Derived numbers (sums, differences, percentiles) are allowed when computed from source figures. Say so in the card subtitle.
